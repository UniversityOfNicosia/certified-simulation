# Contributing

This repository is the curated public release record of a research
program whose development happens in an internal record with its own
review protocol. Because releases must remain byte-anchored to
reviewed, frozen artifacts, **pull requests are not accepted**; any
change to released material would break the hash chain that makes the
releases checkable.

What is welcome, as issues:

- **Replication reports** — you ran a release's certificates or
  rebuilt its PDFs; tell us what you ran, on what environment, and
  what you got, whether it matched or not. Mismatch reports are the
  most valuable ones.
- **Questions** about the results, their declared scopes, or the
  replay procedure.
- **Corrections** — a suspected error in a released paper, proof,
  or certificate. These are triaged against the internal record and,
  when confirmed, addressed in a subsequent tagged release with the
  correction noted; released tags themselves are never rewritten.

Please do not report security-sensitive matters in public issues;
see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
